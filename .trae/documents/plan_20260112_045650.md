1. **错误信息**：`Error: MISSING_MESSAGE: Could not resolve 'article.editor.upload.failed' in messages for locale 'zh'`

   - 但在翻译文件中添加的键名是 `article.editor.upload.failed`
   - 键名不一致导致找不到翻译
